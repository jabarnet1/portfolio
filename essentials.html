Solution & Go-to-Market

Essentials by Adobe: a B2B advisor productivity platform

A solution framework and GTM built for distributed financial advisor and agent networks — modern React/Node.js front end, Adobe Experience Cloud as the integrated backend.

The situation

No true one-stop-shop existed

Distributed advisor and agent networks — captive, third-party, or hybrid — had no unified way to run a personalized, digital-first practice. The alternatives were expensive point solutions or homegrown IT builds, and none of them solved for scale: tens of thousands of unique advisors, most without deep technical skills, each needing their own version of the experience.

What I built

Essentials by Adobe: a collection of B2B solutions advisors and agents could run without deep technical know-how — bridging the gap between their offline relationships and a digital-first client experience. I led the solution architecture and the go-to-market: a modern React/Node.js front end, backed by Adobe Experience Cloud as the integrated platform layer.

The journey framework

Improving the advisor and agent experience end to end

Three stages, each with its own capabilities — not a single generic onboarding flow.

01 · Recruit & Engage

Build the unified prospect profile

  • Merge unknown and known activity with third-party data
  • Generate leads and capture preferences with personalized cross-channel messages
  • Nurture leads with triggered campaigns, surfacing buyer behaviors to improve sales conversations
02 · Apply & Onboard

Streamline data collection and onboarding

  • Dynamic, pre-filled forms and personalized reminders
  • Streamlined document exchange and onboarding data collection
  • Electronic signing for all required advisor and investor documents
03 · Grow & Retain

Drive new advisor success

  • Personalized messaging and self-service support
  • Relevant educational materials to help advisors grow their book
  • Ongoing engagement through the dashboard, not a one-time onboarding push
